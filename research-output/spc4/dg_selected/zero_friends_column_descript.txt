Column descriptions
===================

This file describes the data contained in::

  zero_friends.csv

Each row represents a knot that is a 0-friend of one of the knots in
PS_19. Specifically, these are the 78,507 0-friends in the ZF
collection of Section 5.3.  All of these knots are hyperbolic, as are
their 0-surgeries.  Moreover, the datafile::

  more_zero_friends.csv

has another 79,667 0-friends of the knots in PS_19, namely those where
we have a diagram of at most 90 crossings and where we don't know if
the 0-friend is smoothly slice.

The content of each column is as follows.

* name: The name of the 0-friend knot.  For example, K12n309_0 is the
  first 0-friend of K12n309, and K12n309_1 is its second 0-friend.

* base_knot: The name of the original plausibly slice knot in PS_19
  that it is 0-friends with.

* volume: The hyperbolic volume of the knot exterior.

* tri: An ideal triangulation of the knot exterior.  It is given by
  the triangulation's isosignature, which is accepted by SnapPy and
  Regina.

* num_tets: The number of tetrahedra in the preceding triangulation.

* PD_code: The planar description (PD) code of the simplest known
  diagram for the knot.

* num_cross: The number of crossings in the preceding diagram.

* core_len: The complex length the hyperbolic geodesic in the core of
  the Dehn-filled solid torus in the 0-surgery.  Here "j" is the
  squareroot of -1.
   
* det: The determinant of the knot.

* alex: The Alexander polynomial.

* slice: 1 means the knot is known to be smoothly slice, -1 means it
  is known not to be smoothly slice, and 0 if this is unknown.

* ribbon: Whether the knot is known to be ribbon, using the same
  1, -1, 0 scheme as the slice column.

* base_slice: Whether the base knot is known to be smoothly slice.

* ribbon_to: A dictionary of pairs other_knot:band where applying the
  band to the knot results in other_knot.  For example for K11a58::

    ribbon_to = "{'K6a3': '1609_0_1', 'unknot': '2702_0_-1'}"

  This means applying the compressed band '1609_0_1' to K11a58 yields
  K6a3, whereas the second band gives the unknot. 
 
* ribbon_by_two_bands: 1 means a 2-band ribbon concordance to the
  unknot was found. Unfortunately, we did not record these in detail.
  
* ribbon_by_three_bands: 1 means a 3-band ribbon concordance to the
  unknot was found.

* s_2, s_3: Rasmussen's s-invariant over F_2, and F_3.
  Computed with KnotJob.

* Sq1_odd: The first Steenrod square invariant of the Sarkar-
  Scaduto-Stoffregen *odd* Khovanov homotopy type.  Computed
  with KnotJob.

* bls_odd and Sq1_sum: The s^{\beta_15} and s^\beta invariants of
  Dunfield-Lipshitz-Schuetz, which are defined in terms of
  equivalences classes of local even-odd (LEO) triples.  Computed with
  KnotJob.  These are weaker than the CLS_red LEO invariant computed
  for PS_19 but are easier to compute.
